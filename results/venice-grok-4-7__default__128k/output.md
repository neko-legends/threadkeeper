The summary must let the agent continue without loss. Preserve:

1. The goal and the current state of the work.
2. Every concrete identifier and value that could matter later: names, paths, versions, ports, ids, numbers, dates, commands, commit hashes, vendor details, prices, limits.
3. Decisions made and why. If a value or decision was later changed or corrected, record only the latest one and say clearly that it replaced the earlier one. Do not present superseded values as current.
4. Rules and preferences the user stated that the agent must keep following.
5. Everything still open: unfinished tasks, promises made by either side, pending decisions, things being waited on from other people, and any unresolved interpersonal matters. Say who owes what.
6. What was investigated and ruled out, so it is not repeated.

Rules:
- Use only information that appears in the conversation. Do not invent, guess or round values. If something is unknown, say so.
- Be dense and well organised (short headed sections and bullets are fine). Omit chit-chat and raw logs.
- Quote exact strings (names, paths, commands) rather than paraphrasing them.
